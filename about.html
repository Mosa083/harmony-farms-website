About Harmony Farms

Our Background & History

Located in the Western Cape, South Africa, Harmony Farms began in 2010 as a small family vegetable garden. Over the past decade, it has expanded into a successful 15-hectare farming operation. Today, we proudly supply fresh organic produce to local markets, regional restaurants, and CSA subscribers.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Harmony Farms is committed to growing fresh, organic produce while encouraging people to develop a stronger appreciation for where their food comes from. We promote environmentally friendly farming practices that benefit our community and the environment.

Our Vision

To become one of the region's leading suppliers of locally grown organic produce and inspire other farmers to adopt sustainable methods supporting environmental conservation.

Sustainable Farming Methods

  • Chemical-free cultivation ensuring 100% organic crop output.
  • Responsible soil health improvement and ecosystem restoration.
  • Reduction of environmental footprint through local distribution within a 50km radius.
